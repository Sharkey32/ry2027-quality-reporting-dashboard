Medisolv · CSM Quick Reference · Reporting Year 2027

Three pay‑for‑reporting programs. One shared rule that decides everything.

IQR, Promoting Interoperability, and OQR don't grade performance — they grade whether the hospital showed up. A single missed submission triggers a penalty regardless of quality outcomes, and CMS gives no partial credit.

Rule #1

These are pay-for-reporting programs, not pay-for-performance. The hospital is penalized for failing to submit data — not for what the data shows. That's the single most important thing to communicate to a customer in year one.

Recommendation

Our guidance: work toward getting all Hospital Harm eCQMs up and running in 2027 — HH-PI, HH-AKI, HH-FI, and HH-RF all become mandatory in RY 2028, and HH-VTE follows in 2030. Building the data capture now, ahead of the deadline, spreads the work across multiple years instead of compressing it into one.

Validation applies here too

eCQM validation isn't just an outpatient/OQR concept — CMS already runs it on the inpatient side under IQR. CMS randomly selects up to 200 subsection (d) hospitals, plus targets up to 200 more, for chart-abstracted and eCQM validation each year. Accuracy needs to hold up to re-abstraction, not just pass at submission.

Different calendar

Hybrid measures don't follow the calendar-year cadence used by eCQMs, chart-abstracted, and structural measures. Their measurement period runs July 1 – June 30 instead. The same July–June cadence also applies to the THA/TKA PRO-PM below — worth calling out explicitly so customers don't assume every measure resets on January 1.

Don't forget

PI also requires a full year of eCQM data on all required measures. Meeting the IQR eCQM requirement (see above) automatically satisfies this — it's not a separate submission.

OP-10, OP-32, OP-35, OP-36, OP-39 are claims-based — CMS calculates these automatically with no separate submission.

Always due

Hospitals must submit population and sample size counts for required Abstracted measures and required Web-Based measures — even in quarters with low or zero volume. If a hospital elects to voluntarily report a Web-Based measure like OP-31, those counts become required too, for as long as the hospital participates.

Proposed — not final

The 2027 OPPS Proposed Rule would add eCQM validation to OQR for the first time, mirroring the inpatient validation process. Up to 32 cases/measure would be re-abstracted, requiring a 90% confidence interval upper bound of 75%+ to pass. STEMI (OP-40) would be first in line, since it enters mandatory reporting this same year. Final rule expected November 2026 — treat this as directional, not confirmed, until then.
