2027 Quality Reporting Quick Reference

A guide for Medisolv CSMs covering IQR, Promoting Interoperability, OQR, IPFQR, and Joint Commission ORYX. For each program, see which measures CMS and Joint Commission require for 2027, what Medisolv supports today or is adding, and what to talk through with each client.

Built from the FY 2027 IPPS/LTCH PPS Final Rule (CMS-1849-F) and the 2027 OPPS Proposed Rule (CMS-1850-P). Items marked Proposed are pending the final OPPS rule expected November 2026 — verify before presenting as settled to a customer. This is an internal quick-reference tool, not a substitute for the underlying CMS rule text.

info
Rule #1

The CMS programs here are pay-for-reporting programs, not pay-for-performance. The hospital is penalized for failing to submit data — not for what the data shows.

warning
Recommendation

Our guidance: work toward getting all Hospital Harm eCQMs up and running in 2027 — HH-PI, HH-AKI, HH-FI, and HH-RF all become mandatory in RY 2028, and HH-VTE follows in 2030. Building the data capture now, ahead of the deadline, spreads the work across multiple years instead of compressing it into one.

info
Validation applies here too

eCQM validation isn't just an outpatient/OQR concept — CMS already runs it on the inpatient side under IQR. CMS randomly selects up to 200 subsection (d) hospitals, plus targets up to 200 more, for chart-abstracted and eCQM validation each year. Accuracy needs to hold up to re-abstraction, not just pass at submission.

Threshold

Both require a 70% submission threshold for Core Clinical Data Elements and linking variables (up to 2 missing labs and 2 missing vitals per patient are tolerated).

warning
Different calendar

Hybrid measures don't follow the calendar-year cadence used by eCQMs, chart-abstracted, and structural measures. Their measurement period runs July 1 – June 30 instead. The same July–June cadence also applies to the THA/TKA PRO-PM below — worth calling out explicitly so customers don't assume every measure resets on January 1.

info
No submission needed

OP-10, OP-32, OP-35, OP-36, OP-39 are claims-based — CMS calculates these automatically with no separate submission.

warning
Always due

Hospitals must submit population and sample size counts for required Abstracted measures and required Web-Based measures — even in quarters with low or zero volume. If a hospital elects to voluntarily report a Web-Based measure like OP-31, those counts become required too, for as long as the hospital participates.

error
Proposed — not final

The 2027 OPPS Proposed Rule would add eCQM validation to OQR for the first time, mirroring the inpatient validation process. Up to 32 cases/measure would be re-abstracted, requiring a 90% confidence interval upper bound of 75%+ to pass. STEMI (OP-40) would be first in line, since it enters mandatory reporting this same year. Final rule expected November 2026 — treat this as directional, not confirmed, until then.

Get ready now

Once mandatory, at least 50% of IPF-PAI surveys must be 100% complete (70% beginning 2030) — falling short can trigger the same 2-point reduction. First voluntary quarter is Q4 2027 (due February 15, 2028); first mandatory quarter is Q3 2028 (due November 15, 2028).

info
No submission needed

4 claims-based measures are calculated by CMS directly from claims: Follow-Up After Psychiatric Hospitalization (July 1, 2026 – June 30, 2027), Medication Continuation Following Discharge, 30-Day Unplanned Readmission, and 30-Day ED Visit (each July 1, 2025 – June 30, 2027). The ED Visit window was lengthened for RY 2027 to match the readmission measure.

warning
Always due

The DACA (Data Accuracy and Completeness Acknowledgement) must be signed by August 15, 2028 alongside the annual non-measure and chart-abstracted submissions. Missing it fails the program just like a missed measure.

info
One build, two programs

HH-HYPO, HH-HYPER, PC-02, and PC-07 are also IQR eCQMs, and HBIPS-2 and HBIPS-3 are also required for IPFQR — the same work satisfies both. PC-06 and PC-08 are ORYX-only — neither is part of a CMS program in 2027. PC-06 can be submitted as an eCQM or chart-abstracted.

warning
Alignment is shifting

Joint Commission is moving away from aligning its measures with CMS under Accreditation 360. Requirements used to post in September or October to follow CMS; going forward they may post earlier or on a different schedule. Don't assume a CMS change carries over to ORYX, or the reverse.
